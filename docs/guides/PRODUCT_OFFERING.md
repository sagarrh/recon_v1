# ReconV1

## Turn visibility into action, growth, and measurable revenue impact

ReconV1 helps companies understand **how visible they are, why competitors are gaining attention, what actions they should take, and whether those actions are contributing to business growth**.

It brings competitive intelligence, AI visibility, citation analysis, website evidence, recommendations, and revenue measurement into one product.

ReconV1 answers four important questions:

> **What changed? Why does it matter? What should we do next? Did it create measurable business value?**

## What ReconV1 offers

### AI visibility and citation monitoring

ReconV1 monitors how a company appears in AI-generated answers and how that visibility changes over time.

Clients can see:

- how often their company is mentioned;
- which competitors receive more visibility;
- which questions and topics create opportunities;
- which websites and pages AI platforms cite;
- where citations, mentions, and topic coverage are improving or declining.

### Competitive intelligence

ReconV1 investigates meaningful competitive movement instead of simply displaying numbers.

It brings together Share of Voice, competitor activity, website changes, third-party evidence, and citation signals to identify developments such as:

- a competitor gaining visibility for an important topic;
- a new competitor entering the conversation;
- the client losing mentions, citations, or topic coverage;
- a competitor publishing stronger content;
- an influential source beginning to favour a competitor.

ReconV1 filters out ordinary noise so clients can focus on changes that deserve attention.

### Evidence-backed recommendations

ReconV1 converts important findings into clear actions. Every recommendation explains what happened, why it matters, the supporting evidence, and what the client should do next.

Actions may include:

- creating or improving a priority page;
- strengthening authority and factual proof;
- targeting an underserved question or topic;
- improving company identity information;
- responding to a competitor's successful strategy;
- earning visibility from influential citation sources.

Uncertain findings, missing evidence, and confidence limitations are disclosed instead of hidden.

### Company and page intelligence

During onboarding, ReconV1 creates a reviewed company profile containing the company's products, services, markets, official profiles, contact information, and factual differentiators.

ReconV1 can also inspect important pages to determine:

- whether the company is mentioned;
- whether a page cites or links to the company;
- whether the page has materially changed;
- whether that change may help explain a visibility movement.

Automatically discovered information is treated as a draft until it is reviewed and approved.

### Revenue and business-impact measurement

ReconV1 is being developed to connect visibility and recommendations with measurable business outcomes through Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, and, where available, CRM data.

The objective is to follow the complete journey:

> **ReconV1 signal -> recommendation -> implemented action -> visibility or traffic change -> conversion -> revenue outcome**

Depending on the client's business and available data, ReconV1 will report:

- **actual revenue** from verified purchases or closed deals;
- **influenced revenue** where a ReconV1-tracked page or action participated in the customer journey;
- **modeled expected value** using the client's conversion rate, win rate, average order value, or average contract value;
- **growth indicators** such as impressions, clicks, leads, qualified opportunities, mentions, and citations.

The revenue model will adapt to the client's industry:

- ecommerce businesses can use purchase and transaction revenue;
- SaaS companies can measure trials, demos, pipeline, ACV, and ARR;
- service businesses can measure leads, qualified opportunities, win rates, and engagement value;
- local businesses can measure calls, bookings, and booking value.

ReconV1 will never present an estimated opportunity as earned revenue. Every financial result will show whether it is actual, influenced, or modeled, along with its source, assumptions, confidence, and limitations.

### A unified client experience

Clients receive one clear ReconV1 workspace rather than separate tools, technical logs, or large raw-data files.

The workspace brings together:

- AI visibility and citation performance;
- competitive Share of Voice;
- important gains and losses;
- citation-source opportunities;
- priority recommendations;
- implemented actions;
- traffic and conversion performance;
- revenue impact and confidence;
- relevant evidence and data-quality notes.

Each client can access only its own secure workspace and company data.

## How clients benefit

With ReconV1, clients can:

- understand how their company performs across search and AI discovery;
- detect competitive threats and opportunities earlier;
- know which actions deserve priority;
- replace guesswork with evidence-backed decisions;
- track whether recommendations were implemented;
- measure improvements in visibility, traffic, leads, and conversions;
- connect successful actions to pipeline and revenue where the data supports it;
- explain progress clearly to leadership and stakeholders.

## The result

ReconV1 is more than a monitoring dashboard. It is a **competitive growth and revenue-intelligence system** that connects evidence, decisions, execution, and measurable outcomes.

It moves the client from:

> **"Are we visible?"**

to:

> **"What should we do next, and is it helping the business grow?"**
