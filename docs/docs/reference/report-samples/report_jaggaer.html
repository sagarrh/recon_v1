Methodology

A brief statement of scope and interpretation.

SOV is averaged across all monitored AI answers in the same topic and week. A tracked company absent from an answer is counted as 0% for that answer.

Current SOV, history, and the full dated company leaderboard use one consistent monitored-answer source. Company aliases are consolidated, and a tracked company absent from an answer contributes 0% for that answer before weekly averaging. Results represent presence across monitored AI-generated answers for the supplied topics and reporting period. Share of Voice is not total market share, revenue share, or awareness across every AI platform. Comparisons are limited to the monitored data included in this report.
